about

Hey, I'm Koot, but you can also call me wompkoot! I've been involved in Minecraft staffing since 2023, so I've been doing this for around three years now. During that time, I've had the chance to work in many different staff and management positions across staff, media, and community management. Working in these different areas has helped me learn a lot about how servers work, how teams work together, and most importantly, how to work with people.

When it comes to Staff Management, I've worked with staff teams, helped train and guide new staff, handled staff reports and issues, helped with tasks, and supported staff whenever they needed help. I've also had to make decisions in different situations, deal with conflicts, and make sure things are handled fairly. I always try to be someone my team can rely on and feel comfortable coming to.

For Media Management, I've had experience managing media teams, hosting media events, handling media applications, and helping keep things organised. I enjoy the more fun side of media and like being able to help create things that get people involved and excited about the server.

With Community Management, I've focused a lot on talking with players, helping them when they need it, handling tickets and player issues, listening to feedback, and keeping the community welcoming. I genuinely enjoy talking to people and getting to know the community. I always try to listen to everyone, no matter who they are, and I'm always happy to hear feedback or suggestions. Even if someone has something negative to say, I'd rather listen and learn from it than ignore it.

I'd describe myself as kind, approachable, reliable, patient, and always willing to help. I enjoy working with others and I'm not afraid to ask for help or admit when I've made a mistake. I believe there is always something new to learn, and I'm always open to feedback that can help me improve.

At the end of the day, I do staffing because I genuinely enjoy it. Whether I'm helping a player, supporting a staff member, managing a team, hosting an event, or just having a conversation with someone, I like knowing that I've helped make their experience a little better. I'm always looking to learn, improve, have fun, and make the most out of every opportunity I get.
